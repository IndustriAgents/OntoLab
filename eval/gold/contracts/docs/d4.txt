# Amendment No. 1 to Supply Agreement SA-2026-014

Reference: AMD-2026-014-01
Effective date: 1 July 2026

## Recitals

Brightwater Robotics (the "Supplier") and Northgate Components (the "Customer") entered into Supply Agreement SA-2026-014 dated 1 February 2026 (the "Supply Agreement"). Following the Customer's decision to offer adaptive gripping in its handling cells, the parties wish to add a new product to the Supply Agreement and to adjust its payment terms.

Capitalised terms not defined in this Amendment have the meaning given to them in the Supply Agreement.

## Amendments

With effect from 1 July 2026 the Supply Agreement is amended as follows:

1. A new item 3, the BR-9 Adaptive Gripper Module, is added to the product table in Clause 3 at a unit price of EUR 2,300 and a forecast annual volume of 100 units. Its specification is added to Schedule A.
2. The estimated total contract value over the initial term is increased from EUR 1,250,000 to EUR 1,480,000.
3. Clause 7.1 is deleted and replaced by the following: "The Customer shall pay each undisputed invoice within 60 days of the invoice date." The late-payment interest rate of 8 percent per annum in Clause 7.2 remains unchanged.
4. The delivery period in Clause 4.1 does not apply to the first order of BR-9 modules, which shall be delivered within 45 days.

All other terms of the Supply Agreement remain unchanged and in full force and effect. This Amendment is governed by the same law as the Supply Agreement, namely the laws of England and Wales.

## Signatures
