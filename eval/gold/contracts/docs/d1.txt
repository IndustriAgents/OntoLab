# Supply Agreement SA-2026-014

## 1. Parties

This Supply Agreement (the "Agreement"), reference number SA-2026-014, is entered into between Brightwater Robotics GmbH, a company registered in Germany with its seat in Stuttgart (the "Supplier"), and Northgate Components Ltd, a company registered in England (the "Customer"). The Supplier and the Customer are together referred to as the "Parties" and each as a "Party".

The Customer assembles automated handling cells for the automotive aftermarket and wishes to source motion-control hardware from the Supplier on a long-term basis. The Supplier is willing to manufacture and deliver that hardware on the terms set out below.

## 2. Term and Value

The Agreement takes effect on 1 February 2026 (the "Effective Date") and shall remain in force until 31 January 2029 unless terminated earlier in accordance with Clause 12. On expiry the Agreement renews automatically for successive periods of twelve months unless either Party gives written notice of non-renewal at least 90 days before the end of the then-current term.

The total contract value over the initial term is estimated at EUR 1,250,000. All prices are stated in euro (EUR) and are exclusive of value added tax. Forecast volumes are non-binding and are provided for planning purposes only.

## 3. Products

The Supplier agrees to manufacture and supply the following products to the Customer:

| Item | Product                   | Unit price (EUR) | Forecast annual volume |
|------|---------------------------|------------------|------------------------|
| 1    | BR-7 Servo Actuator Unit  | 1,850            | 160                    |
| 2    | BR-Control Cabinet        | 9,400            | 12                     |

Detailed technical specifications for each product are set out in Schedule A, which forms an integral part of this Agreement. The Supplier shall notify the Customer in writing of any change to a product's specification at least 60 days before the change takes effect.

## 4. Delivery

4.1 The Supplier shall deliver all ordered products to the Customer's plant in Sheffield within 30 calendar days of receiving a purchase order.

4.2 Deliveries are made DAP (Incoterms 2020). Risk in the products passes to the Customer upon delivery at the plant.

4.3 Each delivery shall be accompanied by a delivery note stating the purchase order number, the product item numbers and the quantities delivered.

## 7. Payment

7.1 The Customer shall pay each undisputed invoice within 45 days of the invoice date.

7.2 Late payments bear interest at a rate of 8 percent per annum.

7.3 The Supplier may issue invoices only after delivery of the corresponding products. Disputed amounts shall be notified in writing within 10 days of receipt of the invoice.

## 9. Warranty

9.1 The Supplier warrants that all products will be free from defects in materials and workmanship for a period of 24 months from delivery.

9.2 During the warranty period the Supplier shall repair or replace any defective product at its own cost, including the cost of shipping the replacement to the Customer.

## 12. Termination

12.1 Either Party may terminate this Agreement for convenience by giving not less than 90 days' written notice to the other Party.

12.2 Either Party may terminate this Agreement with immediate effect if the other Party commits a material breach and fails to remedy it within 30 days of being notified of the breach.

12.3 Purchase orders accepted before the date of termination shall be fulfilled in accordance with this Agreement.

## 14. Governing Law

This Agreement and any dispute arising out of or in connection with it shall be governed by the laws of England and Wales. The courts of London shall have exclusive jurisdiction.
