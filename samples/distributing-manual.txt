# Distributing station DS-10

This fictional training station illustrates how a small automated cell
presents a workpiece to another station. All part numbers, timings and signal
assignments belong to the example. The training model describes the flow of
materials and signals between components.

## Layout

The distributing station DS-10 consists of stack magazine MAG-10, transfer
shuttle TS-10 and an output nest. The stack magazine holds six lightweight
practice workpieces. Its guide rails keep the stack aligned above the feed
position. A mechanical retainer supports the upper workpieces while the
bottom workpiece slides out of the stack.

The transfer shuttle carries one workpiece between the feed position and the
output nest. A swivel module SW-10 pivots the carrier between its pickup and
handoff positions. The carrier has a shallow pocket, so its load stays aligned
with the nest while the carrier moves. The output nest supports one workpiece
and remains occupied until the receiving station acknowledges pickup.

## Actuators

The stack magazine has one actuator, feed cylinder CY-10. The feed cylinder
pushes the bottom workpiece into the carrier pocket and retracts before the
shuttle moves. Its stroke is 60 mm. The example regulator setpoint for the
feed cylinder is 350 kPa. The simulator models the feed valve as a single
digital output with an off position that requests retraction.

The transfer shuttle has a swivel actuator ACT-20. The actuator turns the
carrier 90 degrees from its pickup position to its handoff position. It
receives separate digital commands for the two directions. The controller
issues only one direction command at a time. A command ends when the
corresponding end-position sensor reports that the carrier has arrived.

## Sensors and I/O

Magazine sensor SEN-11 detects whether the stack magazine has a workpiece at
the feed position. Carrier sensor SEN-12 detects whether a workpiece entered
the carrier pocket. Feed home sensor SEN-13 reports when the feed cylinder is
retracted. The swivel home sensor SEN-21 and swivel out sensor SEN-22 report
the two end positions of the carrier. Nest sensor SEN-31 reports when the
output nest contains a workpiece.

| Signal | Type | Component | Meaning when active |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| DI_01 | Digital input | SEN-11 | Workpiece present in the magazine |
| DI_02 | Digital input | SEN-12 | Carrier pocket occupied |
| DI_03 | Digital input | SEN-13 | Feed cylinder retracted |
| DI_04 | Digital input | SEN-21 | Carrier at pickup position |
| DI_05 | Digital input | SEN-22 | Carrier at handoff position |
| DI_06 | Digital input | SEN-31 | Output nest occupied |
| DO_01 | Digital output | CY-10 | Extend feed cylinder |
| DO_02 | Digital output | ACT-20 | Turn carrier toward handoff |
| DO_03 | Digital output | ACT-20 | Return carrier to pickup |

## Operating sequence

1. **Check ready.** The controller checks that the carrier is at pickup,
   the feed cylinder is retracted and the output nest is empty. It waits
   for the magazine sensor to indicate a workpiece before starting Feed.
2. **Feed.** The controller extends the feed cylinder. The cylinder moves
   the bottom workpiece into the carrier pocket. The carrier sensor confirms
   arrival before the controller removes the extension command. The example
   timeout for the carrier sensor is 0.8 seconds.
3. **Retract.** The controller waits for the feed home sensor. Retraction
   follows Feed and must finish before the swivel actuator can turn the
   carrier. The example timeout for retraction is 0.6 seconds.
4. **Transfer.** The swivel actuator turns the carrier toward the output
   nest. The swivel out sensor confirms the handoff position. When the nest
   sensor detects the workpiece, the controller signals that the receiving
   station may pick it up.
5. **Return.** After the receiving station acknowledges pickup and the
   output nest reports empty, the swivel actuator returns the carrier to
   pickup. The swivel home sensor confirms that the next cycle can begin.

## Fault handling

If the carrier sensor stays clear after Feed, the controller cancels the
extension command and records a feed timeout. The transfer step stays
disabled until the feed cylinder reports that it is retracted. A feed timeout
and an empty magazine are separate conditions. An empty magazine holds the
station at Check ready and does not set the feed-timeout fault.

The controller records a swivel position conflict when both swivel
end-position sensors are active together. It removes both direction commands
and waits for the simulated operator to clear the fault. If the receiving
station has not acknowledged pickup, the controller keeps the carrier at the
handoff position, even if the nest sensor reports empty.

## Inspection exercises

To inspect the example, trace the path from the stack magazine to the
receiving station. List the actuator that advances a workpiece, the sensor
that confirms it entered the carrier, and the sensor that authorizes the
transfer step. Then compare the signals required at the start and end of the
transfer step. The sensor list can be checked against the I/O table by
following each signal back to its component.

To simulate an empty magazine, set DI_01 to off while the carrier is at
pickup. The controller waits at Check ready. To simulate a feed timeout,
leave DI_01 on and hold DI_02 off through the feed timeout interval. The
second exercise reaches Feed before entering its fault state. The event log
records the operation name, sensor state and elapsed time for either case.
